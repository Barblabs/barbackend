# Each Customer connects its own Slack workspace; every Finding reaches its owner as a DM and the Security Team channel in full

Each Customer installs barbattack's Slack app into its own workspace from the portal's Connect Slack button: OAuth runs in a popup, one installation per Customer, and the bot token is stored encrypted like CMDB credentials (ADR 0012). We use a bot token rather than incoming webhooks because alerts must reach individual Asset Owners by email and a Customer-wide channel; a webhook is bound to one channel and has no identity. The scopes are `chat:write`, `users:read.email` and `chat:write.public`. The middle one resolves Asset Owner emails to Slack users, which a workspace admin may question at consent, but the alternative is asking every owner to link an account by hand. An installation is `active`, `needs re-authorisation` or `removed`; Slack's `invalid_auth` or `token_revoked` on a send flips it to `needs re-authorisation`, so a revoked app never fails silently.

Every Finding the pipeline emits is DM'd to the Asset Owner of its Asset, and the identical message is posted to the Security Team channel. One Finding produces one DM and one channel post; a hundred Findings produce a hundred of each. The channel is not a summary: the Security Team uses it for visibility and as evidence of what was reported, so it mirrors the DMs one for one. Both copies name the Asset Owner's email. That also covers a Finding whose owner cannot be resolved: it still reaches the channel, marked as having no owner, because the channel is the record. The Slack layer owns no schedule and no dedupe. Delivery is queued so a burst of Findings does not hit Slack's per-channel rate limit (ADR 0016). An alert carries the CVE ID, the Asset and a portal link, the Asset Owner's email, the Score and Score Band, why it matters (KEV, EPSS, CVSS, Criticality, Exposure) and the agent's recommendation; the Slack layer never reads Feeds or Scores itself, the pipeline hands it a finished alert.

Alerts fire for every Finding the agent emits, not only on a Score Band change or KEV entry: the pipeline has already filtered, enriched, scored and re-prioritised, so anything that reaches delivery is worth a message. This supersedes the alerting rule in ADR 0009.
